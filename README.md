# DuckSecurity

**Cybersecurity Research · Digital Investigations · Security Tooling**

DuckSecurity is a cybersecurity research identity focused on digital investigations, threat research, security engineering, and practical tooling.

## Focus Areas

- **DFIR** — Digital forensics, incident investigation, and evidence analysis.
- **OSINT & Threat Intelligence** — Infrastructure analysis, digital footprint research, and threat investigation.
- **Anti-Cybercrime Research** — Phishing, online scams, malicious infrastructure, and cyber-enabled fraud.
- **Security Research** — Web security, vulnerability research, and source code analysis.
- **Detection Engineering** — YARA, Sigma, and detection-oriented research.
- **Security Tooling** — Practical utilities, investigative workflows, and automation.

## Research Principles

- Evidence over assumptions.
- Distinguish observed facts from correlations, hypotheses, and attribution.
- Prefer reproducible methods and verifiable sources.
- Document limitations and uncertainty.
- Prioritize defensive research and responsible disclosure.

## Current Focus

Developing practical security tools and investigation workflows, with an emphasis on evidence integrity, transparent methodology, and useful technical documentation.
